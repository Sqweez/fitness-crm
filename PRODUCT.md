# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fitness club front-desk administrators. They work at the reception counter in a brightly lit gym, often with a queue of clients in front of them. Their job on almost every screen: find a client fast, see balance and membership status at a glance, register a visit, sell a service or product.

## Product Purpose

Fit CRM runs day-to-day operations of fitness clubs and club networks: clients, memberships and services, visits, keys, bar and shop, finances, staff. Success is an administrator who never hunts for information: the client card and lists answer "who, what is active, what is owed" in one look.

## Brand Personality

Warm, organized, quick. The interface is a working tool, not a showcase: calm surfaces, clear data, color used to help orientation.

## Anti-references

- Leftover Material Design: saturated header plates with white text, heavy shadows, floating labels.
- Cold generic shadcn/slate look: everything in identical gray-blue bordered boxes, monochrome, no markers. It reads as AI-generated and makes dense screens easy to get lost in.
- Side-stripe accents, nested cards, a box around every element.

## Design Principles

1. Color is navigation. Every section of the client card keeps its own color so it can be found without reading; color always comes with a title.
2. Scan in a second. Tables and lists are optimized for quick vertical scanning: steady rows, aligned numbers, quiet chrome.
3. One system. Sidebar, tables, inputs, panels and buttons share the same tokens; nothing looks borrowed from another library.
4. Structure over decoration. No containers inside containers; a border or fill exists only when it separates something.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and controls. Color is never the only signal: section and status colors always pair with a label. Visible keyboard focus on every control.
